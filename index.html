From Cultivation to Enjoyment.
Coffee plants are grown from seeds in a nursery and then moved to a farm. The plants produce flowers that develop into ripe, red coffee cherries. These cherries are hand-picked to ensure they are fully ripe.
The cherries are hulled to remove the outer fruit and the sweet coating, revealing the seeds, which are the coffee beans. These green coffee beans are then dried in the sun for several days to remove moisture.
The green coffee beans are roasted at high temperatures, a process that transforms them by evaporating moisture, caramelizing sugars, and releasing natural oils. This process gives coffee its aroma and flavor and can affect the final taste of the coffee.
The roasted coffee beans are then ground into smaller particles. Hot water is mixed with the ground coffee in a process called brewing to extract the flavors. The liquid coffee is separated from the used grounds and can be enjoyed as a beverage.
